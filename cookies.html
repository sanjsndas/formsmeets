Cookies Policy

Last updated: March 1, 2025  ·  Effective: March 1, 2025

This Cookies Policy explains how Form Meets Function Journal ("we," "us," or "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website at formmeetsfunction.blog (the "Site"). It explains what these technologies are, why we use them, and your rights to control their use.

In short: We use a minimal set of cookies to keep the Site running smoothly and to understand how readers use our content. We do not use cookies for advertising or to sell your data. You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer, smartphone, or other device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the site owners.

Cookies can be "persistent" (remaining on your device until they expire or you delete them) or "session" cookies (deleted when you close your browser). They can also be "first-party" (set by us) or "third-party" (set by a service provider we use).

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential functionality: to ensure the Site operates correctly, including security features and basic navigation
  • Analytics: to understand how visitors interact with our content, which helps us improve our articles and user experience
  • Preferences: to remember your settings and choices, such as whether you've dismissed a notice

We do not use cookies for targeted advertising, and we do not sell any data collected through cookies to third parties.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

The table below details the specific cookies we use on the Site:

Cookie Name Type Purpose Duration
fmf_session Essential Maintains your session state across page requests. Session
fmf_prefs Preference Stores your cookie consent preferences. 12 months
fmf_analytics Analytics Aggregated, anonymized data about page views and navigation. 24 months
fmf_csrf Essential Security token to protect against cross-site request forgery. Session

We may also use privacy-respecting analytics services that do not rely on cookies or that anonymize IP addresses. These services help us understand readership patterns without tracking individual users.

4. Third-Party Cookies

In some cases, we may use trusted third-party services that set their own cookies. These may include:

  • Hosting providers: for security and performance monitoring
  • Analytics platforms: to measure site traffic and engagement (configured to respect privacy)

We do not control third-party cookies. You should review the respective privacy and cookie policies of those providers for more information.

5. Your Cookie Choices

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can exercise your cookie preferences by:

  • Browser settings: Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can set your browser to refuse cookies or to alert you when cookies are being sent. Check your browser's help section for instructions.
  • Opt-out tools: Some third-party analytics providers offer opt-out mechanisms. Please refer to their policies for details.
  • Consent banner: If you are visiting from a region that requires consent (such as the EEA or UK), you will be presented with a cookie banner where you can accept or manage your preferences.

Please note that if you disable cookies, some parts of the Site may not function properly, and your experience may be affected.

6. Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers include a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature that signals to websites that you do not want to be tracked. Our Site does not currently respond to DNT signals, as there is no consistent industry standard for compliance. However, we do not engage in cross-site tracking for advertising purposes.

7. Changes to This Cookies Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
