Before you write

How long does it take to get a response?
We aim to respond to all inquiries within 5–7 business days. Pitches may take slightly longer during high-volume periods. If you haven't heard back after two weeks, feel free to follow up.
Do you accept guest posts?
Yes, we work with a select number of guest contributors. Please send a detailed pitch with your proposed topic, a brief outline, and links to previous work. We do not accept pre-written articles without a prior pitch.
Can I republish your content?
No. All content is copyrighted. If you're interested in syndicating or excerpting an article, please contact us for written permission. We're happy to discuss partnerships with like-minded publications.
Do you offer design consultations?
We are an editorial publication, not a design firm. We don't offer direct consultations, but our articles are written to help both professionals and homeowners make informed decisions.
